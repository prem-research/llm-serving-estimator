# Prem / Fluso visual assets

The estimator uses the official Prem logomark and Fluso wordmark from
`premAI-io/brand-kit`, revision `29da73f9180510cc975a96d91e50ec454c86781b`.
The four SVGs in `web/assets/` are unchanged copies of the ink and cream variants.
They identify this Prem deliverable; their inclusion does not grant rights to
reuse Prem or Fluso branding for other products.

The stylesheet uses the kit's cream `#FAF9F5`, ink `#0C0C0C`, Alpine Green
`#4D7235`, Petrol Teal `#185771`, and quantitative green ramp. Light and dark
themes select the corresponding logo variants. Form controls, charts, status
messages and keyboard focus retain visible contrast.

Aeonik Pro Regular (400) and Medium (500) load from the existing public font
assets referenced by `https://premai.io`, checked on 8 October 2026:

- `https://framerusercontent.com/assets/1HrGISG4AJ4du80TSJEdeSkEo.woff2`
- `https://framerusercontent.com/assets/NwR5FHLL9KIMS8uSsYcRiSpKPxY.woff2`

The licensed font binaries are not distributed in this repository. Keep them
out of commits and public mirrors of the private brand kit. If Prem replaces
these CDN assets, update the URLs to its new licensed hosting; the browser's
fallback keeps the interface usable during a font-loading failure.

The branding update uses visual assets and tokens. It does not apply the kit's
writing guidelines or change estimator copy, model calculations or calibration.
